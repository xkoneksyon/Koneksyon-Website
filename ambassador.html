Koneksyon Ambassador Program

Represent Koneksyon.
Bring real people in.

Koneksyon Ambassadors introduce the app to new people through their community, social media, events, schools, businesses, and personal network. After approval, each ambassador receives a personal referral link and QR code connected to their campaign.

Share Koneksyon Use your personal QR code or referral link online or in person. You choose how you promote, as long as it follows Koneksyon's rules.
Bring qualified members A referral counts only when a new person uses your referral, completes registration, and is approved by Koneksyon. Raw clicks or unfinished accounts do not count.
Track your progress Your private dashboard shows qualified, pending, and rejected referrals, your campaign goal, dates, and agreed reward.
Earn by campaign Koneksyon sets the target, reward, and campaign period when your application is approved. For example, a campaign could be 2,000 qualified members in 30 days for an agreed payment.

Apply to be an ambassador

Submitting an application does not automatically make you an ambassador and does not create a public Koneksyon profile. Our team reviews your audience, location, platform, and how you plan to represent Koneksyon.

What happens after you apply?

If approved, Koneksyon activates your ambassador account, assigns your campaign goal and reward, and sends you secure access to create your password. Your dashboard then generates your personal referral link and QR code.

Fake accounts, duplicate accounts, existing Koneksyon users, or people who never complete approval do not count toward your campaign.
