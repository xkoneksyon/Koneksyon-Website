Know exactly how it works

Your rate is clearYour approved campaign shows the target, total reward and effective rate per qualified user before you promote.
Your earned referrals are protectedKoneksyon will not reduce the rate retroactively for referrals that already became qualified under your active campaign.
Every paid payout has a receipt referenceWhen Koneksyon marks a payout paid, your dashboard shows the payment reference number so you have a record to use if you need support.
You can question a decisionIf you believe a referral or payout was handled incorrectly, you can contact Koneksyon support and request a review.
