Good to know

A few questions,
answered.

Is Koneksyon only a dating app?

No. Koneksyon brings together personal connection, friendship, opportunities, and community through Intentions, Experiences, and B-Konekt.

Who can join Koneksyon?

Koneksyon is Caribbean-inspired and open to everyone aged 18 and over who meets the platform requirements and community standards.

How does a Konekt request work?

The other person receives a private request. A conversation opens after they accept, so both people choose to connect.

Do I need Premium to get started?

No. You can join for free. Premium adds optional tools, while Konekt+ is a separate visibility feature.
