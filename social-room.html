THE SOCIAL
ROOM

A private social experience.

Some connections happen when the world gets a little quieter.

The Social Room is a new Koneksyon gathering designed around one simple idea: when the room is smaller, there is more space for genuine conversation. Just 30 people, brought together to meet beyond the screen, discover new perspectives, and share an experience that feels personal.

It is about taking the time to say hello, listening to someone else's story, and letting meaningful connections happen naturally. The best moments don't always need a crowd.
